---
name: next
description: Show where Charlie is in the current module and introduce the next step. Use at the start of a session, or when Charlie says "next" or asks what to do.
---

1. Read `progress.yaml`, then the current step's `STEP.md` in `modules/<module>/steps/`.
2. Introduce the step in a few lines:
   - the idea in two to four sentences, using the step's toy example
   - what Charlie writes and where (file and function)
3. Mention `make see N=<n>` to poke at it and `make step N=<n>` to test it.

Don't paste the whole brief, because Charlie can open it. Don't assume they've read the paper.
